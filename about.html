About JCDA

The Jamaica Community Development Association (JCDA) is a non-profit organization dedicated to creating positive change within Jamaican communities. The organization focuses on education, youth empowerment, environmental protection, and community outreach programmes.

JCDA works with volunteers, schools, community leaders, and other organizations to develop projects that improve the quality of life for individuals and families across Jamaica.

Our Vision

To create strong, sustainable Jamaican communities where individuals have access to opportunities that improve their future.

Our Mission

To empower communities through education, youth development, environmental initiatives, and meaningful volunteer participation.

Our Core Values

Integrity

We operate with honesty, transparency, and accountability in everything we do.

Community

We believe in teamwork and supporting each other to create positive change.

Respect

We value every individual and promote equality within our communities.

Sustainability

We support projects that protect Jamaica's environment for future generations.
