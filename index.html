Welcome to JCDA

The Jamaica Community Development Association (JCDA) is a non-profit organization dedicated to improving communities across Jamaica through education, youth initiatives, environmental projects, and community outreach programmes.

Featured Project

Community Tree Planting Initiative

JCDA's Community Tree Planting Initiative focuses on protecting Jamaica's environment by encouraging volunteers to plant trees and promote sustainability within local communities.

Project Achievements

  • 1,500 trees planted
  • 350 community volunteers involved
  • 10 communities supported
