Best Tree Planting JA

Best Tree Planting JA is a community-focused environmental initiative dedicated to increasing Jamaica's green spaces through organized tree-planting activities. The event brings together volunteers, organizations, and local communities to plant trees, promote environmental conservation, and raise awareness about the importance of protecting natural ecosystems. By encouraging collective action, the initiative helps improve air quality, support biodiversity, and create a healthier, more sustainable future for generations to come.

Number of Participants: 1000

Number of Trees Planted: 500
